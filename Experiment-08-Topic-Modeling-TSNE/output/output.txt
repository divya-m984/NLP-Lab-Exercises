t-SNE (t-Distributed Stochastic Neighbor Embedding) to visualize
customer review clusters.

========================================================================
APPROACH
========================================================================

This experiment compares two topic-modeling techniques:
  A. Latent Dirichlet Allocation (LDA) with CountVectorizer
  B. Non-negative Matrix Factorization (NMF) with TfidfVectorizer
Both models extract 4 topics from 60 customer reviews.
The best model is selected by ARI, then NMI, then purity.

========================================================================
DATASET SUMMARY
========================================================================

Total reviews       : 60
Reviews per theme   : 15
Themes              : product_quality, delivery, customer_service, price_and_value
True labels are used only for evaluation, not training.

========================================================================
PREPROCESSING
========================================================================

1. Convert text to lowercase
2. Tokenize with nltk.word_tokenize
3. Retain only alphabetic tokens
4. Remove English stopwords
5. Apply WordNet lemmatization
6. Remove tokens shorter than 2 characters

========================================================================
MODEL A: LATENT DIRICHLET ALLOCATION (LDA)
========================================================================

Vectorizer  : CountVectorizer (stop_words='english', min_df=1,
              max_df=0.95, ngram_range=(1,2))
Model       : LDA (n_components=4, random_state=42,
              learning_method='batch', max_iter=30)

========================================================================
MODEL B: NON-NEGATIVE MATRIX FACTORIZATION (NMF)
========================================================================

Vectorizer  : TfidfVectorizer (stop_words='english', min_df=1,
              max_df=0.95, ngram_range=(1,2))
Model       : NMF (n_components=4, init='nndsvda',
              random_state=42, max_iter=1000)

========================================================================
RESULT
========================================================================

LDA and NMF topic models were applied to 60 customer reviews
across four themes. Both models were evaluated using ARI, NMI,
purity, and silhouette score. The document-topic representation
from the best model (LDA) was visualized using t-SNE.

LDA was selected because it achieved the higher ARI (-0.0076), NMI (0.0444), and purity (0.3500).
Overall ARI, NMI, and purity remained modest. The experiment
demonstrates the topic-modeling and t-SNE workflow, but the weak
scores show that short, overlapping customer reviews are difficult
to separate into clean topics.
