APPROACH DESCRIPTIONS
------------------------------------------------------------------------

A. Keyword Baseline
   Deterministic keyword matching with fixed precedence:
   TREATS > CAUSES > INHIBITS > ASSOCIATED_WITH > NO_RELATION
   Uses predefined keyword groups per relation type.

B. Regex and Pattern-Based Classifier
   Regular expression patterns examining entity order, words between
   entities, passive constructions, and multi-word phrases.
   Returns one of five relation labels based on pattern matches.

C. Multinomial Naive Bayes
   TF-IDF vectorization with unigrams and bigrams (ngram_range=(1,2)).
   Trained on preprocessed training sentences.

D. Logistic Regression
   TF-IDF vectorization with unigrams and bigrams.
   max_iter=1000, class_weight='balanced', random_state=42.
