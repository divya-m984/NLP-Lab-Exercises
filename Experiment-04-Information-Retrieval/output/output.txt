------------------------------------------------------------------------
RETRIEVAL MODELS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. Boolean Term-Matching Retrieval
   Scores documents by the count of matching query terms.
   Awards a bonus when all query terms appear in the document.

B. TF-IDF Vector-Space Retrieval
   Fits a TF-IDF vectorizer on the corpus, transforms queries,
   and ranks documents by cosine similarity.

C. BM25 Probabilistic Retrieval
   Okapi BM25 with k1=1.5, b=0.75.
   Computes term-level IDF and length-normalized term frequency.

D. Latent Semantic Analysis (LSA)
   Applies TruncatedSVD to the TF-IDF matrix and ranks by
   cosine similarity in the reduced latent space.
