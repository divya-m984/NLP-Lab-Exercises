==============================================================================
                                PREPROCESSING
==============================================================================
Shared preprocessing pipeline:
  1. Convert to lowercase
  2. Remove URLs
  3. Remove @mentions
  4. Strip # symbol, retain hashtag word
  5. Tokenize with nltk.word_tokenize
  6. Retain alphabetic tokens only
  7. Remove English stopwords
  8. WordNet lemmatization
  9. Remove tokens shorter than 2 characters

True themes and sentiment labels are NOT used during
preprocessing or clustering. They are used only for
post-hoc evaluation and interpretation.

==============================================================================
                              CLUSTERING METHODS
==============================================================================
A. Word-Based K-Means
   TF-IDF: ngram_range=(1,1), sublinear_tf, L2 norm
   KMeans: k=5, n_init=20, max_iter=500, seed=42

B. Word-and-Phrase K-Means  [REQUIRED METHOD]
   TF-IDF: ngram_range=(1,2), sublinear_tf, L2 norm
   KMeans: k=5, n_init=20, max_iter=500, seed=42
   Uses both unigram and bigram phrase features.

C. Agglomerative Word-and-Phrase
   Same TF-IDF as method B (unigram + bigram)
   AgglomerativeClustering: k=5, cosine, average linkage

Known themes are used ONLY to benchmark discovered clusters
and are NOT provided to the algorithms during training.
