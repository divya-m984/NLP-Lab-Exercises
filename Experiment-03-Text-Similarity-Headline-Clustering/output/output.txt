SIMILARITY METHODS
======================================================================
A. TF-IDF Cosine Similarity
   Builds a TF-IDF matrix from preprocessed headlines and computes
   pairwise cosine similarity between document vectors.

B. Jaccard Similarity
   Compares sets of preprocessed tokens using intersection over union.

C. WordNet Semantic Similarity (Wu-Palmer)
   For each word pair across two headlines, computes Wu-Palmer similarity
   using WordNet synsets. Averages best matches in both directions for
   a symmetric score between 0 and 1.

D. Hybrid Similarity
   Combines 70% TF-IDF cosine + 30% WordNet semantic similarity.
